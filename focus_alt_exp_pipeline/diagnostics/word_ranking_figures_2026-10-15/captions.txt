option_1_subset_comparison
Exclusion prediction after restricting contexts by model-human word-ranking agreement. (A) Spearman and (B) Pearson correlations between predicted exclusion probabilities and observed human rates, computed across the 30 ordered trigger-query pairs in each context and then averaged equally across contexts. Circles indicate No linking and squares indicate Set Top-K. Rows show the full dataset (16 contexts, 480 pairs), contexts with word-ranking Spearman > .9 (7 contexts, 210 pairs), and contexts with word-ranking Spearman = 1 (2 contexts, 60 pairs). Word rankings compare neutral continuation scores with human norming ranks for six tested alternatives. Subsets are nested; points are descriptive means, not confidence intervals. Predictions retain the original corrected held-out evaluation. Pearson measures numerical association, not calibration.

option_2_context_improvements
Context-level changes in exclusion correlation after applying Set Top-K. Each row is one of the seven contexts with word-ranking Spearman > .9. Points show Set Top-K minus No linking for (A) Spearman and (B) Pearson exclusion correlations, each calculated across 30 ordered trigger-query pairs. Diamonds identify the two contexts with perfect word-ranking agreement (bakery and fridge); circles identify the five contexts with word-ranking Spearman = .943 (rounded). Positive values favor Set Top-K. Both panels use identical horizontal limits. Values are descriptive point differences, not significance tests or confidence intervals; predictions are unchanged from the corrected held-out evaluation.

option_3_alignment_and_gain
Word-ranking agreement and the change in exclusion Pearson correlation from No linking to Set Top-K across all 16 contexts. The horizontal axis shows Spearman agreement between the model's ranking and human norming ranks of six tested words. The vertical axis shows the difference in within-context exclusion Pearson correlation (Set Top-K minus No linking), based on 30 ordered trigger-query pairs per context. Blue circles identify contexts with word-ranking Spearman > .9 and < 1; blue diamonds identify the two contexts with perfect agreement. Gray circles show other contexts. The vertical dashed line marks the strict .9 selection cutoff; the horizontal dashed line marks no improvement. Points use their exact coordinates without jitter. Labels use leader lines where needed. The display is descriptive; no trend line or inferential test is fitted.
