Word-ranking agreement in all 16 focus contexts. Each panel plots the six tested alternatives, with human relevance rank (trigger_relevance) on the horizontal axis and Qwen2-7B rank on the vertical axis. Model ranks are calculated within each context from the sampling-array log probabilities used in the HTML viewer. Rank 0 is highest; tied model scores receive average ranks (fan and heater in hot both have rank 0.5). The dashed diagonal indicates identical human and model ranks. Panel annotations report Spearman's rho across the six words, rounded to three decimal places. Contexts are arranged alphabetically.
